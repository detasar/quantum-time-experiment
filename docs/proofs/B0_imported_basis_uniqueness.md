# B0 — Imported Fixed-Partition Basis Uniqueness

## Status

Imported proposition, not a novelty claim of this project.

## Proposition

Under a fixed admissible physical partition, if two or more independently
accessible fragments carry faithful, nondegenerate redundant imprints of the same
clock observable, then the objective clock basis is fixed up to outcome
permutation and local phase convention.

## Assumptions

- The tensor partition and accessible local algebras are fixed before analysis.
- At least two disjoint fragments independently decode the clock outcome.
- Record states are faithful enough to distinguish all clock outcomes.
- The record signature map is nondegenerate.
- Allowed transformations do not include arbitrary global refactorizations.

## Boundary

This proposition selects an unordered objective basis. It does not identify:

- temporal order over the basis labels;
- orientation of earlier versus later;
- a partition-independent clock factor;
- a full relational clock dynamics.

The new work begins at the basis-order-orientation separation and at the
persistent-record poset/capacity/no-go package.

## Rename Test

A simultaneous permutation of clock labels and record labels preserves local
record fidelities. Therefore any result that changes under a pure rename is not
a basis-objectivity result; it belongs to the order/orientation layer and needs
additional assumptions.
